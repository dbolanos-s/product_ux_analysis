Research Limitations
  • Dataset covers Nov 2020 – Jan 2021.
  • Data are aggregated from BigQuery.
  • Initial funnel is based on unique users per event and does not enforce strict within-session event sequence.
  • Observed differences do not establish causality.
  • Product anomalies do not prove UX, price or stock issues.
  • Long-term retention is limited by the observation window.
  • January cohort cannot be evaluated at M1.
